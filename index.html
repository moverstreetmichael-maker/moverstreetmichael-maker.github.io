Crush Through Time

Crush Through Time is an animated YouTube channel. Two seventeen-year-olds, Leo and Nora, wake up in the middle of real historical disasters, and every story is told twice: once from his side and once from hers.

Crush Through Time Uploader

"Crush Through Time Uploader" is a small private tool used only by the channel's owner. It uploads the channel's own finished videos to the channel's own YouTube account, sets their thumbnails and captions, and schedules when they go public. It is not offered to other users.
