Privacy Policy

Crush Through Time Uploader. Last updated: 17 November 2026.

What the app is

Crush Through Time Uploader is a private tool that the owner of the Crush Through Time YouTube channel runs on their own computer. It uploads the channel's own videos, thumbnails and captions to YouTube and schedules their publication. It has no other users.

What data it accesses

With the account owner's permission, given through Google's sign-in screen, the app uses the YouTube Data API with these scopes:

  • youtube.upload: to upload videos to the owner's channel;
  • youtube.force-ssl: to set thumbnails and captions and to check the status of the owner's own videos.

It does not read, collect or store any other Google user data.

How data is stored and shared

The sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is sent to any server other than Google's own APIs. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact details on the Crush Through Time YouTube channel's About page.
